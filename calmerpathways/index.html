What to expect

What you'll experience

Each week builds gently on the one before. There's no rush, no pressure to be anywhere but where you are. This is your pace.

Week One

Where you actually are

Understanding what's going on in your nervous system - not as a diagnosis, just as information. Knowing what's happening is the first step to changing it.

Week Two

Recognising your patterns

Starting to notice the moments that tip you over. Not to judge them - just to see them clearly. When you can see the pattern, you stop being ruled by it.

Week Three

Finding your way back

Simple regulation tools that actually work for your body. Not a one-size-fits-all prescription - your own small toolkit you can reach for when things get heavy.

Week Four

The invisible load

What you're carrying that no one else sees. Making space to name it - not to fix it immediately, but to stop pretending it doesn't weigh anything.

Week Five

Building your pace

What does "steadier" actually look like for your life? Not a perfect version of someone else's routine. Yours. Real, workable, kinder to you.

Week Six

Coming home to yourself

Bringing it together. Not an ending - a landing. You'll leave with your own regulation toolkit and a clearer sense of what you actually need.
