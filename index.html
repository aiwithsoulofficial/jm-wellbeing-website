About Jacqui

Where compassion
meets practical wisdom.

Jacqui McGregor knows what it is to carry more than your share. As a counsellor, coach, and carer herself, she has spent years learning how to create real, lasting change - not through force or willpower, but through compassion, understanding, and practical tools that actually stick.

"You are already the expert on your own life. My work simply helps you remember that."

Her practice is built on a simple truth - and a deep respect for each person's own wisdom. Jacqui works with women (40+) navigating exhaustion and overwhelm, and with young people (16+) finding their footing. Every session is grounded in lived experience, mind-body awareness, and genuine care.
